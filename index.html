Dare to Press – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026 · App package: com.examcache.daretopress

Dare to Press ("the app") is a single-player game made by examcache. This policy explains what the app does with information about you. The short version: the app does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. It does not use the internet to send anything about you anywhere.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves a small file only on your own device. It contains your press count, achievements, unlocked skins, daily dare streak, settings (sound, vibration, voice, graphics), the last time you played, and whether you confirmed you are 18 or older. This file never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it, and you can wipe your progress any time with Settings → Reset progress.

Text-to-speech

If the "Voice" setting is on, the app reads its on-screen lines aloud using your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. The app only sends the game's own lines to that engine; it does not send any information about you. How your text-to-speech engine works is governed by its provider's own policy.

Permissions

The app requests only the vibrate permission, used to make your phone buzz when you press the button. You can turn vibration off in Settings.

Age requirement

The app contains crude humor and strong language and is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
